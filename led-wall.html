LED Wall Configurator FAQs

Which wall settings can I change?

Choose pixel pitch and cabinet model or dimensions. You can also enter per-cabinet pixel values from manufacturer specifications.

How can I set the screen size and curve?

Set the wall by cabinet count or target dimensions, then choose a flat layout or adjust joint angles.

What do the displayed estimates mean?

The tool shows screen size, resolution, and rough weight, power, and port estimates. Check manufacturer specifications before planning or installation.

Do you provide event production with LED walls?

Yes — LED wall rental, setup and on-site crew for concerts, corporate events and launches. Use this calculator to size the wall before the site survey.

Do you build interactive games for events?

Yes — interactive LED games that play in real time on the wall are part of our event production services.

What affects LED wall rental pricing for events?

Pricing depends on screen size, pixel pitch, rental duration and on-site installation. Use this calculator's numbers as the starting point before requesting a quote.
