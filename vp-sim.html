Virtual Production Simulator FAQs

How do I start a simulation?

Use an LED wall configured in the LED Wall Configurator, then upload or paste an image, or choose a sample image.

Which camera settings can I adjust?

Adjust the camera position, lens, and sensor settings while previewing the wall image.

What can I view and save?

Switch between studio and camera views, then save the current camera frame.

Can I plan interactive event games with this?

Yes — preview how an interactive LED game will appear on the actual wall before building it for the event.
